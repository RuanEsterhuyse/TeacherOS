Canonical lesson construction policy

The canonical lesson is an instruction-first consolidation of already validated
TeacherOS stage outputs. It is curriculum-agnostic and may describe any
curriculum or instructional resource.

Preserve validated identity, sequence, objectives, standards, materials,
questions, answers, timing, page references, tasks, annotations, and source
provenance exactly. Never invent quotations, evidence, page mappings,
source-dependent answers, resource content, or curriculum procedures.

Unavailable instructional-resource content must remain explicitly unavailable.
Downstream renderers may format this object but may not add instructional
decisions. The current implementation applies this policy deterministically and
does not invoke a model for canonical construction.
